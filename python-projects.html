Steaming Hot — 2021

A recommendation engine for game developers, using Steam game and player data to study game features, sales, popularity trends, and survival analysis.

Team: Alex Casella, Eduardo Medina, Joseph Leung, Kanishk Jain, Ling Zhou, and Rohit Satija. Mentor: James Bramante.

Are you Van Gogh’s? — 2020

A painting-classification project combining persistent-homology features, face detection, and grayscale histograms to distinguish Van Gogh paintings from other works.

Team: Wing Hong (Joseph) Leung, Francisco Martinez, Jiawei Sun, and Ling Zhou.
