My name is Ling Zhou (周玲). I am an Assistant Professor in the David and Judi Proctor Department of Mathematics at the University of Oklahoma, starting in Aug 2026.

Before joining the University of Oklahoma, I was a Phillip Griffiths Assistant Research Professor at Duke University, mentored by Prof. Ezra Miller. I obtained my Ph.D. in the Department of Mathematics at Ohio State University in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Facundo Mémoli. In Fall 2023, I was a Semester Postdoc at the Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics (ICERM), mentored by Prof. Vladimir Itskov.

Research Interests

  • Algebraic topology, metric geometry, and discrete homotopy theory
  • Applied topology, topological data analysis, and computational geometry
  • Applications in neuroscience, network science, and machine learning
